With its advanced additive technology, it offers unmatched durability, corrosion resistance, and stability — even in high-load, high-temperature, and wet environments.

This ultra-grade formulation ensures long-lasting lubrication and performance consistency for a wide range of industrial and automotive applications.

Key Features & Benefits:

  • High Temperature Tolerance: Performs reliably up to 250°C (482°F) with outstanding drop-point stability.

  • Water and Corrosion Resistant: Provides excellent rust protection and resists washout in humid or wet conditions.
  • Extended Service Life: Reduces relubrication frequency and maintenance downtime.
  • Universal Application: Ideal for wheel bearings, marine equipment, heavy machinery, and industrial tools.
